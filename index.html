CS 499 Computer Science Capstone

Welcome to my Computer Science ePortfolio. This portfolio demonstrates the knowledge and skills I developed throughout the Computer Science program and the improvements I made to my Travlr Getaways full-stack application during CS 499.

The portfolio focuses on three areas of computer science: software design and engineering, algorithms and data structures, and databases.

Professional Self-Assessment

Completing the Computer Science program has helped me develop a much broader understanding of the field and the different career paths available to me. When I first entered the program, I was mainly interested in game development. As I progressed through my courses and learned more about the technology industry, my career goals changed. I became more interested in software engineering, cybersecurity, and information technology.

The projects I completed throughout the program gave me experience in several areas of computer science and helped me determine where my strengths and interests are. Developing this ePortfolio has given me the opportunity to look back at that work and demonstrate how my technical skills have improved throughout the program.

One of the most important things I learned is that computer science involves more than being able to write code. Software development requires planning, communication, problem solving, testing, security, and the ability to understand the needs of other people. Throughout the program, I worked on projects involving full-stack development, mobile applications, databases, artificial intelligence, embedded systems, secure coding, and reverse engineering.

Communication is also an important skill that I developed throughout the program. Technical solutions have to be explained to different audiences, including developers, managers, clients, and users. My coursework required me to document projects, explain design decisions, create diagrams, write technical reports, and describe how applications worked.

Collaboration is another important part of professional software development. Even when completing individual projects, I had to consider how my work would fit into a larger development environment. Organizing code into understandable components, documenting decisions, following programming standards, and using tools such as Git and GitHub make it easier for other developers to understand and contribute to a project.

My coursework also strengthened my understanding of data structures and algorithms. Algorithms provide a structured way to solve problems, while data structures determine how information is stored and accessed. My CS 499 algorithms and data structures enhancement demonstrates this through the addition of sorting functionality to the Travlr Getaways application.

Software engineering has been one of the largest areas of growth for me. One of my largest projects was Travlr Getaways, a full-stack application originally created during CS 465. The project uses MongoDB, Express, Angular, and Node.js. Returning to this project during CS 499 allowed me to examine code I previously created and identify opportunities to improve its structure, reliability, maintainability, and functionality.

Database development has also been an important part of my computer science education. Travlr uses MongoDB and Mongoose to store and retrieve application information through a REST API. During my database enhancement, I expanded the application with trip review functionality. This strengthened my understanding of how databases, backend APIs, and frontend applications work together.

Security is another area that has become increasingly important to me. Through secure coding coursework and other projects, I learned that security should be considered during software design instead of being added only after an application is completed. I gained experience with authentication, authorization, input validation, password protection, and identifying software vulnerabilities.

My experience in the Computer Science program has also helped shape my professional goals. While I originally planned to pursue game development, my experience with different areas of computer science encouraged me to expand my career interests. I am now interested in opportunities involving software engineering, cybersecurity, and information technology.

The artifacts included in this ePortfolio demonstrate this growth through one application that was improved across three areas of computer science. I selected Travlr Getaways because it is a full-stack application that provides opportunities to demonstrate software engineering, algorithms and data structures, and database development.

Together, these enhancements demonstrate my ability to examine an existing application, identify areas for improvement, implement solutions, troubleshoot problems, and evaluate the results. They also demonstrate how different areas of computer science work together within a complete software application.
